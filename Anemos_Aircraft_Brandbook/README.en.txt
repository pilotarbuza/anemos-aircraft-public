ANEMOS AIRCRAFT — BRAND KIT, version 1.0 (October 2026)

00_Брендбук   (Brand book) PDF: idea, voice, the mark and how it is built, logo, clear space,
              color, type, grid, graphics, components, examples.

01_Логотип    (Logo) SVG sources: mark, wordmark, horizontal lockup, lockup with AIRCRAFT,
                    vertical lockup. Versions: color, reverse (for dark or teal backgrounds),
                    mono-dark, mono-white (one ink, no red light).
              PNG: the same on a transparent background, mark at 512/1024/2048 px,
                   lockups at 1000/2000/4000 px.
              PDF: vector files for print.
              Favicon: favicon.ico/.svg, 16–512 px, apple-touch-icon, 1024 px app icon.

02_Цвета      (Colors) anemos-palette.ase (Adobe), .gpl (GIMP, Inkscape, Krita),
              anemos-tokens.css, anemos-tokens.json, Цвета.txt (HEX / RGB / CMYK).

03_Шрифты     (Fonts) Unbounded, Manrope, JetBrains Mono, Doto, plus their OFL licenses.

04_Графика    (Graphics) Grain gradients for backgrounds, streamlines, dot grid,
              color scale for calculation plots.

05_Шаблоны    (Templates) Business card 90×50 mm (PDF, 2 mm bleed), 1080 px avatars,
              1500×500 cover, 1920×1080 slides: title, section, content.

wallpaper     Wallpapers.

Use the logo files as they are. Don't redraw the logo or set it in a font.
